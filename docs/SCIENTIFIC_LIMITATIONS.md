# Scientific limitations

- Outputs describe wild-type structural context only. No mutant 3D structure is generated or predicted.
- Contacts and neighbor counts are observations on the supplied wild-type model, not evidence of changed networks after mutation.
- pLDDT is predicted local confidence of the wild-type AlphaFold model, not mutation-effect confidence.
- Experimental B-factors are never labelled as pLDDT.
- Residue property deltas are coarse descriptors, not energy or function predictions.
- No pathogenicity score, delta-delta-G, docking, or clinical risk prediction is provided.
- Mapping failures fail closed; ambiguous mappings are rejected.
